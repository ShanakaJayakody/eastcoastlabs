## Our Testing Standard

### Independent Third-Party Verification

All East Coast Labs products undergo testing by JanoShik, an independent analytical laboratory. This means:

- **No conflict of interest**: We don't test our own products
- **Published results**: Every batch's Certificate of Analysis (COA) is available for verification
- **Verified purity**: Recent batches have tested between 99.18% and 99.88% purity
- **Transparent chain of custody**: From synthesis to independent verification to your laboratory

### What JanoShik Tests

JanoShik's analysis includes:

- Purity verification via HPLC (High-Performance Liquid Chromatography)
- Identity confirmation via Mass Spectrometry
- Contaminant screening
- Potency assessment

### How to Verify Your Batch

Each East Coast Labs shipment includes:

1. A unique batch number on every vial
2. A corresponding COA with independent test results
3. Instructions for accessing the full JanoShik report

You can cross-reference your vial's batch number against the published COA to confirm you received exactly what was tested.

### Why Independent Testing Matters

In research chemistry, product purity directly impacts experimental validity. Impurities can:

- Introduce confounding variables in experimental design
- Compromise data reproducibility
- Make research results difficult to replicate

By using an independent lab, we ensure that the test results you see represent an unbiased assessment of product quality—not an internal report designed to pass quality control.
