# East Coast Labs

## Who We Are

East Coast Labs is an Australian-owned and operated supplier of research-use-only peptides. Available sizes and pack configurations are shown for each product.

## How We Operate

### Dispatch and Shipping

- **Processing**: Order preparation begins after payment confirmation
- **Shipping locations**: We ship from Australia to Australian addresses
- **Shipping options**: Current rates and free-shipping thresholds appear at checkout
- **Tracking**: All shipments include tracking information

### Packaging

- **Plain packaging**: Orders are shipped in discreet packaging
- **Storage requirements**: Check the supplied product's specifications and contact us about any transit or storage concern
- **Vial protection**: Multiple vial packs are secured to prevent breakage

### Returns

If you receive a product that arrives damaged, incorrect, or otherwise doesn't match your order, contact us promptly with:

- Your order number
- Photos of the received product

We will work with you to resolve the issue. This reporting request does not limit applicable consumer guarantees. See our [returns information](/returns) for the process.

## Research Use Only

All East Coast Labs products are intended for laboratory research use only. These products are not intended for human consumption, therapeutic use, or veterinary application.

Researchers purchasing from East Coast Labs must:

- Have appropriate laboratory facilities and training
- Comply with all local regulations regarding research chemicals
- Follow institutional safety protocols for handling peptides

We do not provide dosing information, administration guidance, or therapeutic advice. Our role is to supply materials for controlled research environments.
