Peptides are sensitive molecules. Once you have paid for verified, high-purity material, storage is what protects that quality between delivery and use. The two states — lyophilised powder and reconstituted solution — have different requirements.

## Lyophilised (unreconstituted) vials

In freeze-dried form, peptides are relatively stable, but they are not indestructible. The enemies are **heat, moisture and light**.

- **Keep sealed until use.** The stopper protects against moisture ingress. Lyophilised powder is hygroscopic — it draws in water from humid air, which accelerates degradation.
- **Store cool and dry.** Refrigeration extends stability. For longer-term storage, a freezer is preferable for many compounds.
- **Protect from light.** Store vials in their packaging or a dark container.

Handled this way, lyophilised research peptides remain stable for extended periods — often many months to years depending on the specific compound.

## Reconstituted solution

Once dissolved, the clock speeds up. Peptides in solution are far more reactive.

- **Refrigerate.** Store the reconstituted vial at fridge temperature, not at room temperature.
- **Use bacteriostatic water** for reconstitution where a solution will be stored, not plain sterile water. The benzyl alcohol preservative suppresses microbial growth over the storage window.
- **Keep it sealed and use aseptic technique.** Every time the stopper is pierced there is a small contamination risk; a clean swab and a fresh, sterile needle mitigate it.
- **Avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles.** Freezing and thawing a solution repeatedly stresses the peptide. If long-term storage of a solution is required, aliquoting into single-use portions before freezing is preferable to freezing and thawing one vial many times.

## Temperature at a glance

| State | Short term | Longer term |
|---|---|---|
| Lyophilised powder | Refrigerated | Frozen |
| Reconstituted solution | Refrigerated | Aliquot & freeze |

## Why storage protects your results

A degraded peptide does not announce itself — the powder still looks the same. But partial breakdown lowers the effective purity of the material you actually use, reintroducing exactly the variability that buying a high-purity, [independently tested batch](/lab-results) was meant to eliminate. Good storage is the last step in keeping a verified batch verified.
