MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-derived peptide studied widely in metabolic and cellular-energy research. This overview covers what it is, from a research perspective.

## What MOTS-c is

MOTS-c (**Mitochondrial ORF of the twelve S rRNA type-c**) is a short peptide encoded within the mitochondrial genome — one of a small class of **mitochondrial-derived peptides (MDPs)**. Unlike most peptides, which are encoded in nuclear DNA, MOTS-c originates from a mitochondrial gene, which is part of why it draws interest in cellular-energy research.

At East Coast Labs, [MOTS-C](/product/mots-c) is supplied lyophilised with an independently published [Certificate of Analysis](/lab-results) for every batch.

## Areas of scientific research interest

Published literature has explored MOTS-c in experimental models related to:

- Mitochondrial signalling and cellular-energy regulation
- Metabolic homeostasis and adaptive-response research
- Intracellular signalling-pathway studies
- Mitochondrial-derived peptide interaction research
