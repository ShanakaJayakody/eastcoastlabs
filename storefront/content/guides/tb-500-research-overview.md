TB-500 is a synthetic peptide frequently studied alongside BPC-157 in tissue-repair research. This overview covers what it is and the scientific contexts in which it appears.

## What TB-500 is

TB-500 is a **synthetic peptide** related to a naturally occurring region of **thymosin beta-4 (Tβ4)**, a protein involved in cell migration and actin regulation. TB-500 corresponds to an active fragment of that protein and is used as a stable, synthesisable reference compound in laboratory research.

At East Coast Labs, [TB-500](/product/tb-500) is supplied lyophilised with an independently published [Certificate of Analysis](/lab-results) per batch.

## Areas of scientific research interest

Preclinical literature has examined thymosin beta-4 and related fragments in experimental models of:

- Cell migration and actin-cytoskeleton dynamics
- Tissue-repair and regeneration research
- Angiogenesis signalling
- Extracellular-matrix remodelling studies

TB-500 is one of the two compounds in our [Recovery & Repair stack](/stacks), paired with [BPC-157](/product/bpc-157) — the combination most often seen together in connective-tissue research.

> These describe *research contexts* only — not efficacy claims. TB-500 is not approved for therapeutic use by any regulatory authority.
