GLOW and KLOW are multi-peptide research blends that combine three individually studied peptides in a single preparation. This overview covers what they are, from a research perspective.

## What the blends are

Both [GLOW](/product/glow) and [KLOW](/product/klow) are research-formulated blends of the same three peptides — **[BPC-157](/product/bpc-157), [TB-500](/product/tb-500) and [GHK-Cu](/product/ghk-cu)** — presented together in a single lyophilised vial. They differ in total peptide mass per vial (GLOW 70&nbsp;mg, KLOW 80&nbsp;mg). Each is supplied with an independently published [Certificate of Analysis](/lab-results).

Combining peptides in one preparation lets researchers study **complementary pathways** without reconstituting and handling three separate vials.

## Why blends are studied

The three component peptides are each the subject of established preclinical literature:

- **BPC-157** — connective-tissue repair and angiogenesis research ([overview](/learn/bpc-157-research-overview))
- **TB-500** — cell migration and tissue-repair research ([overview](/learn/tb-500-research-overview))
- **GHK-Cu** — dermal and extracellular-matrix research ([overview](/learn/ghk-cu-research-overview))

Together they are studied in models of connective-tissue repair, cellular signalling, and regenerative research pathways — the rationale being potential **complementary interactions** between compounds acting on related pathways.

> These describe *research contexts* only, not efficacy claims. Neither blend is approved for therapeutic use, and neither is supplied for human or animal consumption.

## Physical & handling properties

- **Format:** lyophilised powder (three-peptide blend)
- **Reconstitution:** dissolve gently in [bacteriostatic water](/learn/reconstituting-research-peptides) to a clear solution; use the [concentration calculator](/learn) to record the total-peptide concentration
- **Storage:** refrigerate lyophilised and reconstituted vials ([storage guide](/learn/storing-research-peptides))

## Verifying a blend

A blend's **Certificate of Analysis** is especially important — you want confirmation that all three peptides are present at the stated purity, not a single peptide sold as a blend. Every East Coast Labs batch is independently tested and published before listing, [verifiable by batch number](/lab-results). See [How to Read a COA](/learn/understanding-a-certificate-of-analysis).
