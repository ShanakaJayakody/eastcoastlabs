Melanotan-2 (MT2) is a synthetic melanocortin analogue studied in pigmentation and receptor-signalling research. This overview covers what it is, from a research perspective.

## What MT2 is

Melanotan-2 is a **synthetic analogue of α-melanocyte-stimulating hormone (α-MSH)** — a cyclic peptide that acts on the melanocortin family of receptors. Its structure makes it more stable than natural α-MSH, which is part of why it is used as a reference compound in melanocortin-receptor research.

At East Coast Labs, [MT2](/product/mt2) is supplied as a lyophilised powder with an independently published [Certificate of Analysis](/lab-results) for every batch.

## Areas of scientific research interest

Published literature has explored Melanotan-2 in experimental models related to:

- Melanocortin-receptor (MC1R–MC5R) signalling studies
- Pigmentation and melanogenesis research
- Peptide-receptor binding and structure–activity research
- Neuroendocrine signalling models

> These describe *research contexts* only, not efficacy claims. MT2 is not approved for therapeutic use and is not supplied for human or animal consumption.

## Physical & handling properties

- **Format:** lyophilised white-to-off-white powder
- **Class:** cyclic synthetic melanocortin-analogue peptide
- **Reconstitution:** dissolve gently in [bacteriostatic water](/learn/reconstituting-research-peptides) to a clear solution
- **Storage:** refrigerate lyophilised and reconstituted vials, protected from light ([storage guide](/learn/storing-research-peptides))
