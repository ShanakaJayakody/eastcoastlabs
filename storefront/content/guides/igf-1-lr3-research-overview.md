IGF-1 LR3 is a modified insulin-like growth factor analogue used as a stable reference compound in cellular-growth and signalling research. This overview covers what it is.

## What IGF-1 LR3 is

IGF-1 LR3 (**Long R3 Insulin-like Growth Factor-1**) is an engineered analogue of IGF-1. Two modifications distinguish it: an **arginine substitution at position 3** and an **added N-terminal extension**, which together reduce its binding to IGF-binding proteins and increase its stability in solution — useful properties for controlled laboratory research.

At East Coast Labs, [IGF-1 LR3](/product/igf) is supplied lyophilised with an independently published [Certificate of Analysis](/lab-results) per batch.

## Areas of scientific research interest

Published literature has explored IGF-1 and its analogues in experimental models related to:

- Cellular growth, proliferation and differentiation research
- Insulin-like growth factor receptor signalling studies
- Metabolic and regenerative research models
- Growth-factor binding-protein interaction studies

It is included in our [Longevity & Cellular collection](/collections/longevity-cellular) alongside other cellular-research compounds.

> These describe *research contexts* only, not efficacy claims. IGF-1 LR3 is not approved for therapeutic use and is not supplied for human or animal consumption.

## Physical & handling properties

- **Format:** lyophilised white powder
- **Class:** modified insulin-like growth factor analogue
- **Reconstitution:** dissolve gently in [bacteriostatic water](/learn/reconstituting-research-peptides); avoid shaking
- **Storage:** refrigerate lyophilised and reconstituted vials; avoid repeated freeze-thaw ([storage guide](/learn/storing-research-peptides))

## Purity and verification

Because it is an engineered analogue, confirming **identity** by mass spectrometry matters as much as purity — you want the correct modified sequence, not standard IGF-1. Both are reported on the batch **Certificate of Analysis**, independently tested and published before listing, [verifiable by batch number](/lab-results).
