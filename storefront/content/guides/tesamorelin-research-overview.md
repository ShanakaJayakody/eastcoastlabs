Tesamorelin is a synthetic growth-hormone-releasing-hormone analogue studied in neuroendocrine and metabolic research. This overview summarises what it is, from a research perspective.

## What tesamorelin is

Tesamorelin is a **synthetic analogue of growth-hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH)** — a stabilised version of the natural signalling peptide that regulates growth-hormone release from the pituitary. Its structure makes it a common reference compound in endocrine-signalling research.

At East Coast Labs, [Tesamorelin](/product/tesamorelin) is supplied lyophilised with an independently published [Certificate of Analysis](/lab-results) per batch.

## Areas of scientific research interest

Published literature has explored tesamorelin in experimental models related to:

- Endocrine signalling and hormonal-regulation research
- Growth-hormone-releasing-hormone analogue interaction studies
- Neuroendocrine and pituitary-mediated signalling research
- Metabolic-regulation research models

> These describe *research contexts* only, not efficacy claims. Tesamorelin is not approved for therapeutic use and is not supplied for human or animal consumption.
