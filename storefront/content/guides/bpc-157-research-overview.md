BPC-157 is one of the most widely studied peptides in preclinical research. This overview summarises what it is and the scientific contexts in which it appears, strictly from a research perspective.

## What BPC-157 is

BPC-157 is a **synthetic pentadecapeptide** — a chain of 15 amino acids (sequence GEPPPGKPADDAGLV). It is derived from a partial sequence of a protein found in gastric juice, from which its name ("Body Protection Compound") originates. As a small, stable peptide it has become a common reference compound in laboratory studies.

At East Coast Labs, [BPC-157](/product/bpc-157) is supplied as a lyophilised powder with an independently published [Certificate of Analysis](/lab-results) for every batch.

## Areas of scientific research interest

Published preclinical literature has explored BPC-157 in experimental models related to:

- Connective-tissue and tendon repair research
- Angiogenesis (blood-vessel formation) signalling studies
- Gastrointestinal mucosal protection models
- Cytoprotection and cellular-response research

It is frequently paired with [TB-500](/product/tb-500) in tissue-repair research — the combination is available as our [Recovery & Repair stack](/stacks).

> These are descriptions of *research contexts* only. Nothing here is a claim of efficacy, and this compound is not approved for therapeutic use by the TGA, FDA or any regulatory authority.

## Physical & handling properties

- **Format:** lyophilised (freeze-dried) white powder
- **Class:** synthetic peptide, 15 amino acids
- **Reconstitution:** dissolves readily in [bacteriostatic water](/learn/reconstituting-research-peptides); the solution should be clear
- **Storage:** refrigerate lyophilised vials; refrigerate and seal after reconstitution — see the [storage guide](/learn/storing-research-peptides)
