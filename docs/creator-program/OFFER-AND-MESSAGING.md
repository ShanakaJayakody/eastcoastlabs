## Positioning

**Your influence. Our next chapter.**

Build a partnership with East Coast Labs, one stage at a time. Start with product rewards, share in your own voice, and grow towards commission and ongoing support.

The program is a selective relationship with a clear progression. Lead with belonging and creative independence, then explain the exchange plainly. A$300 is the maximum value of the first product, never a cash fee, joining bonus or earnings claim. Use “product rewards” because posting is part of the exchange; calling the first vial an unconditional gift would hide that commitment.

Keep **ECL Creator Collective** as the program name and **Apply to the collective** as the main CTA. “Creator” is the preferred public term; it respects the participant's craft while audience reach remains a selection factor. Preserve ECL's precise, considered voice: no income hype, manufactured scarcity, invented partner endorsements or promises of unlimited supply.

## Recommended pilot practice

The following are ECL-specific recommendations to agree in each brief, not confirmed public promises or published industry benchmarks.

**Select for consistent audience fit.** Ask shortlisted creators for platform screenshots covering a recent 30-day period, audience geography/age and their last 10 comparable organic posts. Review median views alongside average views so one viral post does not drive the decision. Note content type, publication age and any paid promotion. Consider an engaged smaller creator where the audience is relevant; do not treat follower count as proof of reach. TikTok's own creator evaluation guidance includes audience demographics, median video views, engagement and recent content. [TikTok creator evaluation guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/best-practices-for-creators-to-be-discovered-for-brand-collaboration?lang=en)

**Define qualified attention without inventing a metric.** Use the platform's actual names for views, reach, watch time and engagement. Assess these alongside the relevant audience's geography and age; where the platform cannot show a breakdown, mark it unavailable. Do not multiply unrelated age and geography percentages to fabricate a qualified-view count. Do not add video plays across posts and call the result unique people. Separate paid and organic results. Exclude bought engagement and investigate suspicious spikes before selection. Agree any reach benchmark before posting.

**Brief three distinct stories.** Choose complementary concepts within the permitted campaign scope: a brand introduction, a considered detail or question, and an audience conversation. Give creators room to choose hooks, framing and language. Define whether a Reel, TikTok or static post qualifies; do not count one cross-posted asset three times by default. Stories can support the campaign without silently replacing agreed primary posts. No personal-use demonstration, efficacy testimonial or health-benefit narrative is part of the brief.

**Set a manageable timeline.** A suggested pilot is three posts across 30 days from confirmed delivery, with dates agreed around the creator's schedule. Collect a seven-day insights snapshot for each post, plus live links. Recommend one consolidated feedback round and a response within five business days. Confirm the second-vial selection and dispatch window in advance; a suggested service target is dispatch within five business days after completion is verified. These timings require operational capacity before being promised.

**Protect trust and creator ownership.** Agree product choices, delivery costs, scope, publication dates, availability, disclosure, review turnaround, cancellation and content rights before sending the first product. Reserve the reward stock or agree an equivalent replacement with the creator. Do not demand a positive review. Licence any brand reposting by channel and duration; paid advertising, account access, exclusivity, raw files and likeness reuse require separate agreement. Gifts supplied in exchange for posts are commercial incentives that must be disclosed clearly. [ACCC influencer disclosure action](https://www.accc.gov.au/media-release/photobookshop-pays-penalties-for-influencer-reviews)

**Make referral decisions explainable.** Review delivery/reliability, relevant organic attention, engagement quality, audience interest and commercial potential. Use profile/site visits, saves, substantive comments and enquiries where available, without collecting private audience messages or health information. Compare results to the creator's own baseline and the brief's agreed criteria. Record whether to invite, offer a revised collaboration or conclude the pilot. A referral invitation should follow evidence and sustainable economics, not just three published posts.

**Account for the true cost.** Track product cost for both vials, both deliveries, fulfilment and staff time. Retail value is useful for communicating the creator reward, but is not ECL's acquisition cost. Before setting a discount and commission, calculate eligible net revenue after discounts/refunds and applicable tax, then subtract commission, product/fulfilment/payment costs and the cost of continued rewards. Set a cohort budget before acceptance. Consider a small initial cohort and review completed collaborations before expanding; do not advertise an invented number of available places.

**Make ongoing support concrete.** Agree the products, review cadence and posting commitments in a partner plan. A monthly check-in and quarterly review are sensible pilot defaults. Gifts should reinforce a valuable relationship, with agreed limits and reliable delivery. Communicate changes prospectively and honour rewards already earned.

## Referral setup before activation

Before an invitation becomes active, agree a commission rate and audience discount; eligible products/orders; the commission base; code/link precedence and attribution window; refund, cancellation and self-referral treatment; vesting and payout dates; and termination handling for earned balances. Tie commission to confirmed paid eligible sales, never checkout submission alone. Provide creators with understandable statements and a correction route.

This update describes the later opportunity. It does not implement referral tracking, discount eligibility, a commission ledger, a creator dashboard, automated shipments or payouts. Those require separate commerce implementation and verification. An admin accepting an application remains a review decision, not a shipment or agreement.

## Product and publication scope

The TGA's April 2026 advisory names unapproved peptides including GHK-Cu and says promotion through social media or influencer channels is likely to breach advertising laws. It also explains that research-use disclaimers do not remove advertising or supply obligations. Confirm product-specific supply and promotion eligibility before publishing this product-based recruitment offer, dispatching rewards or commissioning public posts. Removing a health claim, adding a disclosure or changing compensation does not establish permission. This work is a local draft of the owner's commercial model, not a clearance decision. [TGA peptide advisory](https://www.tga.gov.au/safety/safety-monitoring-and-information/safety-alerts/understanding-your-responsibilities-when-importing-compounding-and-supplying-unapproved-peptide-products)

If the proposed peptide promotion is not permitted, resolve the eligible product and campaign scope before activation. Do not reinterpret a research label as permission for personal use. Keep operational review details here rather than interrupting the application journey with internal implementation notes.
